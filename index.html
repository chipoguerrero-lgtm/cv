ProfilePerfil

Educator with a degree in Pedagogy, more than 30 years in education and nearly two decades teaching English as a foreign language, from A1 to C1, in primary, secondary and university settings in Querétaro. I teach English for communication and academic immersion at B2 level, design courses in Mexican Sign Language, and teach oral and written expression in Spanish. My classes are built on active, communicative and content-integrated learning (CLIL), with metacognition at the center, universal design for learners who are Deaf or have low vision, and a critical, guided use of artificial intelligence that supports — never replaces — the learner’s own thinking.
